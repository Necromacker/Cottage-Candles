Our Story

Welcome to Cottage Candles, a 100% pure homemade brand. We craft each candle with passion and attention to detail to help create the ultimate aesthetic and sensory experience for your home.

Founded in 2025, our mission is to deliver serenity directly to your doorstep. What started off as a small passion project quickly flourished into a fully-fledged brand beloved by thousands of people looking to embrace the warmth and tranquility of our curated selection.
